# MATLABApps
The '3-Stream Heat Exchanger App' folder contains one app authored by Aravind Suresh and Nicole Donovan in the chemical engineering department at Manhattan University. The app allows users to design or rate a co-current 3-stream heat exchanger by the LMTD method or the NTU-Effectiveness method.  
The 'Flow Through Pipes Apps' folder contains two apps authored by Faith Figetakis, Kevin Lynn and Anika Wahid in spring 2023 as part of an assignment for their chemical engineering senior laboratory course at Manhattan University.
